# Deal&Drive campaign artwork

All three assets were generated with the built-in Imagegen tool on 2026-10-14.
They are original decorative campaign artwork. They are never substituted for
dealer photos, uploaded media, vehicle offers, or API image results.

| File | Intended use | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `src/assets/vehicles/campaign-coupe.webp` | Buyer hero and authentication | 1774 × 887, WebP quality 88, about 125 KB |
| `src/assets/vehicles/campaign-interior.webp` | Sera / dealership editorial story | WebP quality 86, about 211 KB |
| `src/assets/vehicles/campaign-suv.webp` | Dealer hero and pricing editorial | WebP quality 86, about 235 KB |

Generation prompts:

- Coupe: photorealistic premium automotive campaign photograph, unbranded graphite
  silver sports coupe in a dark concrete studio, low front three-quarter camera,
  full vehicle on the right with negative space for the headline, restrained warm
  orange architectural light, realistic metal reflections; no people, logos, text
  or watermarks.
- Interior: photorealistic luxury cockpit detail, graphite leather, sculptural
  steering wheel and seat, brushed dark metal console, real stitching, restrained
  warm orange ambient lighting; no people, logos, readable text or watermarks.
- SUV: photorealistic graphite performance SUV, front three-quarter view outside a
  contemporary concrete showroom at dusk, silver and black palette, warm orange
  showroom light, entire vehicle visible; no people, logos, text or watermarks.

The former dealer hero was removed after its consumers were replaced. The older
`drivedeal-hero.png` remains because the existing inventory adapter references it;
that adapter and its data behavior were kept outside the visual cleanup.
